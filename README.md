# Hey,👋 I'm **Theogene Habimana, PhD**

🎓 Postdoctoral Researcher in Finance and Applied Economics at [Hanken School of Economics](https://www.hanken.fi/).  
My research examines how government borrowing affects firms' access to finance, using firm-level and macroeconomic data to study domestic and external public debt, corporate borrowing, and financial conditions across countries.

## 🧪 Current Research Projects

- **Sovereign Debt in Sub-Saharan Africa Project** *(ongoing)* — Research collaborator on a project sponsored by the Center for the Study of African Societies and Economies (CSASE) at Harvard University. The project examines sovereign debt accumulation, fiscal sustainability, public debt management, and the economic consequences of government borrowing in Sub-Saharan Africa. My focus is the relationship between sovereign borrowing, private-sector credit, and corporate financing.
- **Rethinking Capital Structure Theories in the Age of Rising Public Debt: Crowding-Out versus Crowding-In** *(ongoing)* — Global panel of ~100 countries and more than 55,000 firms over 1990–2024, studying how domestic and external government borrowing shape firms' financing decisions, with attention to institutional quality, financial development, and cross-country differences.
