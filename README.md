Hello, I'm Theogene Habimana, PhD 👋
Postdoctoral Researcher | Finance and Applied Economics  
Hanken School of Economics, Helsinki, Finland  
Academic Website · LinkedIn · ORCID · CV
My research examines how government borrowing affects firms' access to finance. I use firm-level and macroeconomic data to study domestic and external public debt, corporate borrowing, and financial conditions across countries. My current research includes cross-country work on government debt and capital structure and a collaboration on sovereign debt in Sub-Saharan Africa through a project sponsored by the Center for the Study of African Societies and Economies (CSASE) at Harvard University.

📌 Current Research Projects
Sovereign Debt in Sub-Saharan Africa Project (ongoing)  
Research collaborator on a project sponsored by CSASE at Harvard University, examining sovereign debt accumulation, fiscal sustainability, public debt management, and the economic consequences of government borrowing in Sub-Saharan Africa. My interests within the project include sovereign borrowing, private-sector credit, and corporate financing.
Rethinking Capital Structure Theories in the Age of Rising Public Debt: Crowding-Out versus Crowding-In (ongoing)  
Global panel analysis covering approximately 100 countries and over 55,000 firms (1990–2024). The study investigates how domestic and external government borrowing relate to corporate leverage, accounting for institutional quality, financial development, and cross-country differences.
